## What changed

A hard capability gate now rejects prime editing (single-pegRNA PE2/PE3)
when the variant's edit span exceeds `PE_MAX_EDIT_SPAN_BP = 50`. Before
this change, PE was ranked first for HDR-required large structural
deletions despite being technically incapable of executing them.

### Behavioural contract

- **Single-step PE.** When a variant exceeds the PE capability limit, a
  `Strategy` object is still emitted with the name `"Single-step Prime
  Editing"`, but with `rejection_reasons` populated and
  `modality_prior_score = 0`. `strategy.is_rejected` is therefore True,
  and the `StrategyScorer.rank()` loop skips it — PE does not get
  scored or ranked, but it is surfaced to downstream UI as a considered
  option with a clear reason for exclusion. This is the "visible
  exclusion" behaviour requested by the review.
- **Dual PE.** Silently skipped when either variant in the pair exceeds
  the limit.
- **Hybrid PE + HDR.** The PE-target variant determines the gate. The
  hybrid is generated for the orientation where PE lands on the
  PE-capable variant, and skipped for the orientation where PE lands
  on the gated one.
- **BE, HDR, EXON\_DELETION.** Unaffected. BE capability (transition-only,
  window-position) was already enforced upstream via
  `FeasibilityLabel.NOT_FEASIBLE`.

## Threshold justification

`PE_MAX_EDIT_SPAN_BP = 50` is a conservative cap for single-pegRNA PE2/PE3:

- **Insertions** are bounded by the RT-template length of the pegRNA
  scaffold. Anzalone et al. (Nature, 2019) demonstrated ≤44 bp
  insertions using PE2/PE3.
- **Deletions** in the same single-pegRNA regime extend to roughly
  ≤50–80 bp in practice.
- **Larger structural edits** (hundreds of bp or whole-exon deletions)
  require twin-prime / PASTE architectures (Anzalone et al.,
  Nat. Biotechnol., 2022). These are a distinct editor construct, not
  single-pegRNA PE, and are not represented in the current modality
  enumeration.

The threshold is defendable and parameterizable; changing it requires
editing one module-level constant. Any future change should be justified
against the same literature base.

**Case-level before → after on the 4 previously-failing HDR-required cases:**

| Case | Pre-fix top-1 | Post-fix top-1 | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| `HDR_DMD_016` (180 kb exon deletion) | PE | **HDR** | PE gate fires, HDR is only viable candidate |
| `HDR_NF1_017` (70 bp deletion) | PE | **HDR** | PE gate fires, HDR only |
| `HDR_FBN1_022` (~8 kb multi-exon) | PE | **HDR** | PE gate fires, HDR only |
| `HDR_COL7A1_020` (compound het, 2×1 bp transitions) | PE | **BE** | **Not directly caused by the gate** — both variants are 1 bp and PE is not gated. The post-fix ranking elevates BE to top-1, which satisfies the `acceptable=[hybrid base editing + hdr]` truth label. Mechanism of the ranking shift is not fully audited in this session. |

**Note on the COL7A1 result.** The review anticipated Fix #1 would lift
top-1 accuracy from 26/30 to 29/30 (96.7%), because COL7A1 was known
to be a compound-het ordering issue rather than a PE-capability issue.
The observed lift is 30/30 (100%), meaning COL7A1 also flipped to an
acceptable answer. This is a favourable outcome but the pathway is
not fully traced; a future session should confirm whether this is a
stable property of the scorer or a benchmark-fixture artefact. **Do
not cite "Fix #1 → 100%" in the paper without that audit.**

## Out of scope for this fix

Two items from REVIEW_NOTES_2026-04-18.md remain untouched:

- **Fix #2** — fold bystander severity into the safety dimension
  instead of the post-TOPSIS consequence penalty (~2 days, architectural
  cleanup with identical numerics).
- **Fix #3** — surface Dirichlet-sampled rank stability at inference
  time (~3 days, addresses "feels redundant" perception).

Related but distinct issue observed during this session:

- **Compound-het handling.** The generator produces single-mutation
  strategies for each variant of a compound-het case and ranks them
  independently. A strategy that addresses only 1 of N pathogenic
  variants should be penalized for incompleteness. This is not an
  artefact of Fix #1; it is a separate architectural gap worth
  addressing before claiming compound-het coverage in the paper.
